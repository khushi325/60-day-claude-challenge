    Financial literacy

    Tips, checklists & resources

    The fundamentals that compound over a student budget and beyond.

    Money tips for students
    • Track every source of income — mixed income (allowance + job + stipend) is easy to undercount.
    • Automate a small transfer to savings the day money arrives, before you can spend it.
    • Use student discounts on software, transit, and streaming — they add up over a semester.
    • Separate "needs" (rent, food, transport) from "wants" (entertainment, takeout) so budget cuts are easier decisions.
    • Review subscriptions every semester — cancel what you haven't used in 30 days.
    • Build a small emergency buffer (even $150–300) before optimizing anything else.

    Ask your AI assistant

    Try prompts like:

    • "Review my spending this month and flag anything unusual."
    • "Help me build a budget for a semester abroad."
    • "What's a realistic emergency fund size for a student?"
    Good habits to build

    Pay yourself first, review your budget weekly (5 minutes is enough), and negotiate or shop around for recurring costs like phone plans once a year.

    When to ask for help

    If debt, medical bills, or family financial pressure ever feel overwhelming, most campuses offer free financial counseling — use it before it becomes a crisis.
