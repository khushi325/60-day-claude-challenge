# 🧠 Day 36 – Cognitive Pattern Explorer with Claude AI

## 📌 Overview

On **Day 36** of the **60 Days Claude AI Challenge**, I built and explored an interactive **Cognitive Pattern Explorer** using Claude AI.

This simulator is designed to help users understand how different thinking patterns influence decision-making in everyday situations. By completing interactive activities in both Calm Mode and Stress Mode, users can compare how their cognitive responses change under different emotional states and receive a personalized Reflection Journal.

The experience combines psychology, critical thinking, and AI to promote greater self-awareness and better decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Objective

- Understand different cognitive thinking patterns.
- Compare decision-making in Calm Mode and Stress Mode.
- Improve self-awareness through interactive reflection.
- Identify personal thinking tendencies.
- Learn how emotions influence reasoning.
- Analyze personalized AI-generated insights.

---

# 🧠 About the Simulator

The Cognitive Pattern Explorer is an interactive educational application that helps users explore how they think and make decisions in different situations.

The simulator includes:

- Calm Mode & Stress Mode
- Everyday scenario-based challenges
- Drag-and-drop thinking activities
- Thinking sequence organization
- Personalized Reflection Journal
- Thinking profile comparison
- Replay with different cognitive modes

---

# 🔄 Simulation Workflow

The simulation follows these stages:

1. Choose Calm Mode or Stress Mode
2. Complete Chapter 1: Everyday Scenarios
3. Finish Chapter 2: Drag-and-Drop Activities
4. Arrange Thinking Sequence in Chapter 3
5. Review Personalized Reflection Journal
6. Compare Thinking Tendencies
7. Replay in the Opposite Mode

---

# 📖 Chapter 1 – Everyday Scenarios

In this chapter, users respond to realistic daily-life situations to observe how different emotional states influence their decisions.

The simulator evaluates:

- Decision-making approach
- Emotional awareness
- Logical reasoning
- Response patterns

---

# 🧩 Chapter 2 – Interactive Activities

This section includes drag-and-drop exercises where users organize thoughts, emotions, and possible responses to different scenarios.

Skills explored include:

# 🔄 Chapter 3 – Thinking Sequence

Users arrange the correct sequence of cognitive steps involved in solving a situation.

The simulator analyzes:
