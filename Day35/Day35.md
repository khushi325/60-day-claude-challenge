# 🕵️ Day 34 – Marketing Detective with Claude AI

## 📌 Overview

On **Day 34** of the **60 Days Claude AI Challenge**, I built and explored an interactive **Marketing Detective** application using Claude AI.

This simulator transforms marketing analysis into a detective-style investigation where users examine campaign evidence, organize clues, identify marketing mistakes, and review expert explanations. Through randomized cases and interactive evidence boards, the application makes learning marketing strategy both engaging and practical.

---

# 🎯 Objective

- Learn how to analyze marketing campaigns.
- Identify common marketing mistakes.
- Investigate campaign evidence using logical reasoning.
- Improve strategic marketing decision-making.
- Understand the impact of marketing errors on business performance.
- Learn through interactive case studies.

---

# 🕵️ About the Simulator

The Marketing Detective Simulator places users in the role of a marketing investigator responsible for solving campaign failures.

The simulator includes:

- Theme selection
- Randomized marketing investigations
- Interactive investigation board
- Drag-and-drop evidence cards
- Campaign analysis
- Expert verdict and explanation
- Marketing Learning Report
- Replay functionality with multiple cases

---

# 🔄 Investigation Workflow

The simulation follows these stages:

1. Choose Your Preferred Theme
2. Accept a Marketing Investigation
3. Review Campaign Background
4. Analyze Marketing Evidence
5. Drag & Organize Evidence Cards
6. Identify the Main Marketing Mistake
7. Review Expert Verdict
8. Study the Marketing Learning Report
9. Replay with New Marketing Cases

---

# 🔍 Investigation Board

The investigation board contains evidence related to campaign performance, including:

- Audience Insights
- Campaign Analytics
- Budget Allocation
- Customer Feedback
- Engagement Metrics
- Conversion Data
- Creative Performance
- Marketing Channels

Users organize these clues to determine the root cause of campaign failure.

---

# 📊 Common Marketing Mistakes

The simulator helps identify issues such as:

- Poor Audience Targeting
- Weak Value Proposition
- Incorrect Platform Selection
- Ineffective Content Strategy
- Low Customer Engagement
- Budget Misallocation
- Inconsistent Branding
- Weak Call-to-Action (CTA)

---

# 📈 Marketing Learning Report

After completing the investigation, the simulator generates a report containing:

- Investigation Score
- Accuracy Rating
- Correct Marketing Mistake
- Expert Explanation
- Improvement Suggestions
- Key Marketing Lessons
